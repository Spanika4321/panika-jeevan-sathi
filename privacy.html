🙏 Our Privacy Promise

We aim to provide a respectful matrimonial environment where members can control the personal information they choose to share.

Please remember that anything you voluntarily share with another member may be seen or saved by that person.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, you may provide information such as:

  • Name and profile information
  • Community information
  • Age and city
  • Profile and cover photos
  • Interests and preferences
  • Messages and community posts
  • Email address used for account access

2. Profile Information

Only add information that you are comfortable sharing on a matrimonial platform.

Do not publish sensitive information such as passwords, OTPs, bank details, government identification numbers or financial information.

3. Photos & Posts

Profile photos, cover photos, stories and posts may be visible to other members according to the platform's current visibility settings.

Do not upload another person's photograph without their permission.

4. Private Messages

Messages are intended for communication between members. Always use good judgement before sharing your phone number, address or other personal details.

If another member behaves suspiciously, use the Report & Safety feature.

5. Safety & Fraud Prevention

  • Never share your OTP or password.
  • Never send money to someone you met online.
  • Be careful with requests for financial help.
  • Report suspicious or abusive profiles.
  • Block members who make you uncomfortable.

8. Reporting Privacy Concerns

If you believe your information is being misused, or if you encounter suspicious behaviour, please use the Report & Safety section or Contact page.
