About us

A matrimonial service built for our community

Families often struggle to find suitable proposals within the community through word of mouth alone. PANIKA JEEVAN SATHI keeps the same respect and family involvement, but makes the search organised: every member has a proper profile, families can search with clear filters, and contact happens only after both sides agree.

The service is run for the community, not as a business. There is no premium membership, no payment gateway and no locked profile anywhere on this website.

What we promise

  • Free forever — registration, profiles, search, interests, shortlist and messaging.
  • Privacy first — you control your photo, contact number and search visibility.
  • Community focused — membership is for the Panika, Manikpuri, Kabirpanthi and Adivasi communities.
  • Human moderation — every reported profile is reviewed by our team.
  • No advertising pressure — no pop-ups selling you a “premium upgrade”.

How to use the service well

Complete the profile honestly

Accurate age, education, occupation and family details save everyone's time.

Add a recent photo

Profiles with a clear photo receive significantly more interests.

Respond to interests

Accept or decline promptly — families appreciate a clear answer.

Involve the family early

Use messaging to introduce families, then meet in person with elders present.

Talk to us

Questions, suggestions or help with registration — we reply free of cost.
