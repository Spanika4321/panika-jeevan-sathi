Privacy policy

Last updated:

1. What we store

We store only what is needed to run a matrimonial service: your account email, a securely hashed password, your display name, your profile details (age, education, occupation, family and community details, partner preferences), your uploaded photo, and the interests, shortlists, messages and notifications that you create on the site.

2. What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your data to third parties.
  • We never show your email address on a public profile.
  • We never publish your phone number without your permission — you control it in Privacy settings.
  • We do not run advertising trackers on this website.

3. Who can see your profile

You choose the visibility of your profile: everyone, registered members only, or hidden. You can also hide your photo, hide your contact number, or switch yourself off from search results at any time from Edit profile → Privacy settings.

4. Messaging

Private messages are visible only to you and the other member. Messaging opens after an interest is accepted. Messages may be reviewed by moderators only when a member reports abuse.

5. Security

Passwords are stored using a strong salted key-derivation function (scrypt) — never in plain text. Sessions use signed, expiring cookies. Reset and verification links are single-use and time-limited.

6. Your rights

You can edit your profile at any time, change your password, and delete your account permanently from Settings. Deleting your account removes your profile, photo, interests, shortlist, messages and notifications.
