Frequently asked questions

Is this website really free?

Yes. Registration, profile creation, search, interests, shortlist, messaging and notifications are free. There is no payment gateway and no premium plan.

Who can register?

Members of the Panika, Manikpuri, Kabirpanthi and Adivasi communities.

How do I message someone?

Send an interest from their profile. Once they accept, the message button opens and you can chat privately.

How do I hide my profile?

Open Edit profile → Privacy settings and choose “Hidden”, or turn off “Show me in search results”.

Someone is asking me for money. What do I do?

Never send money. Report the profile from their profile page (Report button) and message us on WhatsApp immediately.
