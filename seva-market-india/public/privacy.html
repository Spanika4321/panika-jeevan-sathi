Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

What we collect

For customers: name, email, phone, city and the service address you enter for a booking. For professionals: the same contact details plus business name, category, area, experience and the description you publish. Passwords are stored only as a salted scrypt hash.

How we use it

Your details are used to create your account, match you with professionals in your city, send booking updates and keep the marketplace safe. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising networks.

Who can see it

A professional sees only the name, city, address and note needed to complete your booking. Your email and phone are never shown publicly. Administrators can see account records to investigate reports and enforce the terms.

Cookies

One HTTP-only session cookie keeps you logged in. It stores a random token, not your password or personal details, and it is removed when you log out.

Keeping your data

Account and booking records are kept while your account is active so that booking history and reviews remain available. You may ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time by writing to support.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, pages are served over HTTPS, and state-changing requests must come from this site. No system is perfectly secure — please use a strong, unique password and tell us immediately if you suspect misuse.

Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: support@sevamarketindia.in.
