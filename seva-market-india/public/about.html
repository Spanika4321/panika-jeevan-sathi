About us

Built for India's neighbourhood services

Millions of skilled people — plumbers, electricians, technicians, cleaners, tutors — run small businesses that depend on word of mouth. SEVA MARKET INDIA gives them a storefront, and gives families a single place to find someone trustworthy, at a fair and visible price.

Our mission

Make home and family services as easy to book as a cab — with prices you see before you commit and professionals you can hold accountable afterwards.

How we verify

Every professional submits identity and address proof, a service category and real work history. Profiles stay flagged until the checks are complete, and customers can see the verification status before they book.

How we earn

The platform is free for customers. Professionals keep what they earn; a small platform fee is agreed with them separately. There is no membership fee, no forced lead purchase and no hidden charge added to your bill.
